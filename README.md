One sentence, 214 characters, pitching the worst, most useless app imaginable: **Voicemail Ballet** — an app that converts every voicemail into step-by-step interpretive dance instructions, so you can finally experience your landlord's eviction notice as a moving piece of modern art.

`main.py` (standard library only) writes the pitch to `/output/entry.txt`.

## Entry

> Voicemail Ballet is an app that converts every voicemail you receive into step-by-step interpretive dance instructions, so you can finally experience your landlord's eviction notice as a moving piece of modern art.
